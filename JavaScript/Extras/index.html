Our Legacy

A century of automotive excellence

The Beginning

Bayerische Motoren Werke (BMW) was founded in Munich, Germany. Originally producing aircraft engines for World War I.

First Motorcycle

BMW introduced the R32, the first motorcycle produced by BMW, establishing the company's reputation for engineering excellence.

Automotive Entry

BMW began automobile production after acquiring the Eisenach plant, launching the Dixi, the first BMW car.

The New Beginning

The BMW 700 marked a turning point, saving the company from financial crisis and establishing the sporty BMW identity.

BMW M Founded

BMW M (Motorsport) was established, creating high-performance vehicles that would become legendary.

Electric Future

BMW leads the industry in electric mobility with the i4, iX, and upcoming Neue Klasse platform.

Facts & Curiosities

Discover what makes BMW unique

Aviation Roots

BMW started as an aircraft engine manufacturer. The blue and white logo represents a spinning propeller against the Bavarian sky.

Power Unit Innovation

BMW is one of the few manufacturers that produces engines, transmissions, and electronics all in-house.

Racing Heritage

BMW has won the 24 Hours of Le Mans, DTM championships, and numerous Formula 1 races throughout history.

Sustainability

BMW was the first German car manufacturer to commit to sustainable production, with plans for carbon neutrality by 2050.

The "Ultimate" Promise

BMW's slogan "The Ultimate Driving Machine" has been used since 1970 and has become one of the most iconic automotive taglines.

Engine Philosophy

BMW's iconic straight-six engines are renowned for smoothness and performance, a philosophy dating back to 1933.
